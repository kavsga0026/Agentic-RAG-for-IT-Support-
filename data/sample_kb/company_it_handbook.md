# Lumora Logistics — Employee IT Help Guide

Version 3.2 · Owned by the Lumora IT Service Desk · Reviewed quarterly

This guide covers the most common IT questions from Lumora staff. If your issue is not covered here, raise a request in the Lumora Help Portal or call the Service Desk on extension 4400 (Monday–Saturday, 7:00–21:00).

---

## Remote Access (Lumora Connect)

Staff working from home, a client site, or while travelling must connect through **Lumora Connect**, the company's remote access client, before opening any internal application such as the warehouse dashboard, HR portal, or shared drives.

How to connect:

1. Open Lumora Connect from the system tray (Windows) or menu bar (macOS).
2. Choose the region closest to you: *India-South*, *India-North*, *Singapore*, or *Europe-West*.
3. Sign in with your Lumora email address.
4. Approve the sign-in request in the Lumora Verify app on your phone.

If Lumora Connect will not connect:

- Check that your home or hotel internet works by opening any public website.
- Quit Lumora Connect completely and reopen it.
- Switch to a different region.
- Public Wi-Fi in airports and cafés sometimes blocks remote access. Use your phone's hotspot instead.

Never turn off antivirus, the firewall, or device security software to get connected. If none of the steps above work, raise a Help Portal request and attach a screenshot of the error message and the region you selected.

Lumora Connect is installed automatically on company laptops. Personal devices are not allowed to use Lumora Connect.

## Passwords and Account Lockouts

Lumora account passwords must:

- be at least **16 characters** long,
- not contain your name, username, or the word "Lumora",
- not reuse any of your last 10 passwords.

Passwords expire every **180 days**. You will receive email reminders 14 days and 3 days before expiry.

To change or reset your password, use the **Lumora Account Centre** (account.lumora.internal). It works from inside the office network or while connected through Lumora Connect.

After **six** incorrect sign-in attempts, your account locks for **30 minutes** and then unlocks automatically. If you are still locked out after that, or you have forgotten your password and cannot use the Account Centre, call the Service Desk. They will verify your identity using your employee ID and a callback to your registered phone number.

The Service Desk will **never** ask for your password or a Lumora Verify approval code. If anyone asks for either, even someone claiming to be from IT, refuse and report it to security@lumora.example.

## Lumora Verify (Multi-Factor Authentication)

Two-step verification is required for email, Lumora Connect, the HR portal, finance systems, and any administrator account.

Approved verification methods:

- **Lumora Verify app** push notification (preferred)
- **Lumora Verify app** six-digit code (when your phone has no data connection)
- **FIDO2 security key** (required for administrators and finance staff)

Text message (SMS) codes are not an approved method.

If you get a new phone, set up Lumora Verify on it **before** resetting or giving away the old one. Open the Account Centre, go to *Security*, and choose *Move Lumora Verify to a new device*. If you have already lost access to your old phone, call the Service Desk to have your verification reset after an identity check.

If you receive a Lumora Verify approval request you did not trigger, tap **Deny** and report it immediately. Repeated unexpected requests usually mean someone has your password.

## Installing Software

All approved applications are available in the **Lumora App Store** on your company laptop. Installation from the App Store does not need administrator rights.

If the software you need is not listed:

1. Submit a *New Software Request* in the Help Portal.
2. Include the software name, the business reason, and how many people need it.
3. Your manager approves the request.
4. IT Security reviews licensing, data handling, and security risk. This normally takes **5 working days**.

Browser extensions follow the same rule: only extensions listed in the App Store are permitted.

Local administrator rights are only granted to staff in engineering and IT roles, and never just to install a single application.

## Office Wi-Fi

Lumora offices provide two wireless networks:

| Network | Who uses it | Access |
|---|---|---|
| **Lumora-Staff** | Company laptops and phones | Connects automatically using your company device certificate |
| **Lumora-Guest** | Visitors and personal devices | Daily password posted at reception; internet only |

Company laptops should never be connected to Lumora-Guest. If your laptop does not join Lumora-Staff automatically, restart it while in the office. If it still fails, its device certificate may have expired; raise a Help Portal request.

## Printing

Printers in all Lumora offices use **follow-me printing**. Print to the queue called *Lumora-Print*, walk to any printer in the building, and tap your ID badge on the reader to release your job.

- Jobs not released within **24 hours** are deleted.
- Colour printing is limited to 200 pages per month per person.
- Documents labelled *Confidential* must not be left at the printer.

If your badge is not recognised at the printer, your badge may need to be re-registered at reception.

## Suspicious Emails and Phishing

Warning signs of a phishing email:

- urgent requests to pay an invoice, change bank details, or buy gift cards,
- links that ask you to sign in to a Lumora system,
- sender addresses that look almost, but not exactly, like a Lumora or partner address,
- unexpected attachments, especially `.zip`, `.html`, or macro-enabled Office files.

If you suspect phishing, use the **Report Phish** button in Outlook. Do not forward the email to colleagues, and do not reply to the sender.

If you have already clicked a link or entered your password:

1. Change your password in the Account Centre immediately.
2. Call the Service Desk and say you may have entered your credentials on a phishing page.
3. Do not delete the email; Security may need it.

## Lost, Stolen, or Damaged Devices

**Lost or stolen laptop or phone:** Report it to the Service Desk within **one hour** of noticing, and inform your manager. Provide the asset tag (on the sticker under the laptop) if you know it, and where and when you last had the device. If it was stolen, also file a police report and send the reference number to the Service Desk.

IT will lock the device remotely and, if it is not recovered within 48 hours, erase it. Do not try to track or recover the device yourself.

**Damaged device:** Raise a Help Portal request with photos. A loan laptop is available from the IT desk at each office while yours is repaired. Back up anything not already saved to Lumora Drive, because repairs may require a full reset.

## Storing and Sharing Files

Save work files to **Lumora Drive**, the company cloud storage, not to the laptop desktop or downloads folder. Files on Lumora Drive are backed up nightly and kept for 90 days after deletion.

Rules for sharing:

- Share with colleagues by adding their Lumora email address.
- External sharing links expire after **30 days** and require the recipient to verify their email address.
- Customer data and employee personal data must not be shared outside Lumora without approval from the Data Protection team.
- Personal USB drives are blocked on company laptops. Encrypted company USB drives can be requested through the Help Portal.

## New Joiners and Leavers

**New joiners** receive their laptop and Lumora account on their first day. On that day they must:

1. set a new password in the Account Centre,
2. set up Lumora Verify,
3. complete the 20-minute *Security Basics* training in the Learning Hub within the first week.

**Leavers** return their laptop, phone, badge, and any security keys to the IT desk on their last working day. Account access ends at 18:00 local time on that day. Managers must submit the leaver request at least **5 working days** before the last day.
